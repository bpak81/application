# MHS — The Client Intelligence Map

**Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Ben's ruling (today): seven subjective areas must be covered on every client. Everything else comes from the objective diagnostic — the PPLD.** This doc is the master checklist: every question in the application, onboarding sittings, and calls must serve one of these seven (or the ongoing layer); anything that serves none gets cut. **Bold = Ben's words verbatim or near-verbatim**, with source.

**The seven:** 1 History · 2 Lifestyle · 3 Obstacles (real or perceived) · 4 Expectations · 5 Constraints · 6 Capacity · 7 Timelines

**The PPLD (objective):** genetics · bloods · urine · stool · sleep · stress · strength · VO2 · posture/structural balance · bodyfat

## 3. OBSTACLES — real or perceived (opposite treatments)

**Ben's rule (Jul 13): perceived = beliefs, dismantled with data. Real = realities, designed around. "A perceived constraint treated as real becomes a permanent excuse; a real constraint treated as perceived becomes a blown plan. The sort is the skill."**

Perceived:
- **"What do you believe might hold you back here?"** → Sitting 3
- **"When you imagine this not working, what's the story you tell?"** → Sitting 3
- **"Anything about you that makes you a harder case — genetics, age, metabolism, history?"** → Sitting 3
- **Where does HE believe his biggest obstacles are** (Sep 28 §4) → Sitting 3
- What's held him back in past attempts → Application ("how did it go?")

Real (verified, then routed to §5 Constraints or to the PPLD as hidden-blocker tests — mold, metals, inflammation):
- Surfaced the same way, sorted by the coach post-call.

## 6. CAPACITY — the 4 Capacities + willing & able

**Subjective reads (the PPLD provides the objective side):**

| Capacity | What we must know | Ben's words / source |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | How he responds to being pushed; **push or hate to push**; **how he defines a great workout** — "Tell me about an amazing, memorable workout. How did you feel when you were done?" (**energized vs. beaten = very different approaches**) | Sep 28 §6 |
| Psychological | **Does he get stressed easily — how high is his neuroticism**; **relationship with hard things — "like them or avoid them; probably situational"** | Sep 28 §4 |
| Physiological | **How well does he tolerate stress; how much training stress can he take**; **total stress load — financial, emotional, environmental, internal**; **stress resilience** — "some people go through enormous stress and do not feel stressed" (feels-vs-carries gap) | Sep 28 §2 |
| Metabolic | Eating pattern + regimentation (subjective side of what bloods/stool/urine verify) | Sep 28 §7 |

**Willing & able (the design envelope):**
- **Honest daily time budget — "the most you'll actually give on a normal weekday, not your best week"** (26 §2a) → Sitting 3
- How often he WANTS to train; time he HAS per session (Sep 28 §6) → Sitting 2
- **When life gets busy, what breaks first — and why** → Sitting 3
- **"What are you committed to changing? Be specific."** (Aug 3, Ben's line — written commitment) → Application
- Knowledge × Action archetype (Operator/Coiled/Grinder/Restless) + calibration vs. self-rating (false-certainty flag) → Application free-texts, confirmed on call

## The PPLD — the objective ten (per Ben, today)

genetics · bloods · urine · stool · sleep · stress · strength · VO2 · **posture/structural balance** · bodyfat

- Posture/structural balance = the gym session with Ben: **joint-by-joint, every plane and range, clean = "crisp and sharp," left-right balance "extremely important,"** what he does well and doesn't. Every client.
- Scheduling and windows per `16` Part 5 table; **"program design never waits on the full ten"** — the Now-set + the seven areas is sufficient for the first protocol.
- Hidden blockers surfaced in §3 (mold, metals, inflammation) route into PPLD add-ons via the functional doc.

## Ongoing layer (post-onboarding — the weekly check-in keeps these seven current)

DT5 adherence + where the missed day broke · week-over-week 8-state · forced ranking even on perfect weeks · win attribution · **"what are you avoiding, minimizing, or not telling me?"** · next-7-days risk + pre-decision · Push/Plan/Perspective · monthly 10-areas re-grade + sustainability flag · quarterly Gap Score re-rate. (26 §4 — live in this repo as the weekly sheet.)

## Rule

Every form question, call question, and diagnostic must map to one of the seven areas, the PPLD, or the ongoing layer. Anything that maps to none is cut. Anything on this map not captured somewhere is a gap — currently there are none; the three sittings + gym session + application cover all seven.
