# PART A — FROM THIS CONVERSATION

## A1 · Sep 28 — Onboarding content spec (Ben's eight sections)
1. **Physical assessment** (exists): move well, no pain, clean movement joint by joint — clean = crisp and sharp; every plane and every range available; left-to-right balance extremely important; current and past injuries.
2. **Stress tolerance and recovery**: how well does he tolerate stress; how much training stress can he take; total stress load — financial, emotional, environmental (toxins), internal (something in the body); stress resilience — how stressed does he feel ("some people go through enormous stress and do not feel stressed"); sleep; wearables — does he use any.
3. **Life and profession**: profession; work — purposeful, challenging, heavy, or manageable.
4. **Mindset and communication**: does he get stressed easily / how high is his neuroticism; how does he want us to communicate / how directly can we call him out; what does he do when he is being lazy; relationship with hard things — likes or avoids, probably situational; how does he turn off; where does he believe his biggest obstacles are.
5. **Lifestyle and relationships**: healthy lifestyle AND healthy relationship with it; principle — the relationship with a thing matters more than the thing itself (drugs/pharmaceuticals not simply good or bad); relationship with food, training, stress, relationships, money.
6. **Training**: psychological relationship with exercise — push or hate to push; how he defines a great workout — "Tell me about a time you had an amazing, memorable workout. How did you feel when you were done?" (energized vs. beaten = very different approaches); desired frequency; available time; years of experience.
7. **Nutrition**: current eating habits + times of day; food types; off-plan foods and frequency — never "cheat"; alternative: "How regimented are you, 1–10? How often do you eat things that are not good for you?"; allergies.
8. **Medical and supplements**: supplements; pharmaceuticals; peptides; any diagnosis mental or physical; any major illness or disease.
+ Dave precedent: gym session with Ben, every client — Ben assesses the body, what he does well and doesn't.
+ Rules: shortest possible; exact time per section.

## A3 · Oct 28 — The 8 areas (supersedes A2's seven) + Defining Success + cumulative rule
"The onboarding/the calls should be cumulative for information gathering."
1. **History**: family history, athletic, health, fitness, stress, successes/failures, relationship with the desired outcome and its process.
2. **Health**: current challenges, complaints, illnesses, nervous system state, mindset state.
3. **Lifestyle**: schedule, commitments, time, decisions, values, daily operating system.
4. **Obstacles**: time, family, location, access, money, support, work — what will stop your success?
5. **Constraints**: injuries, weakness, pain, fitness, imbalance, mindset, where you operate relative to capacity — what will slow you down?
6. **Expectations**: goals, timelines, communication type, communication frequency, support.
7. **Timelines**: 30, 90 day, 12 month, 36 month targets.
8. **Capacity**: training experience, fitness, strength, form, limitations, energy, mindset, stress resilience.

**Defining Success (Ben's module):** How do you define success? Whose definition is it? ("The path to fulfillment and purpose is formed in silence, and forged under pressure.") Success is not how I or anyone defines it — not society, not your parents, not your friends; find it for you — usually along peace, satisfaction, significance, contribution. Why is this important to you? Why now? What are you actually chasing — goals vs. the avoidance of suffering? What are your personal standards? What does winning look like — goals fit into look, feel, or perform; how do you know when you've won? Short-term vs. long-term success. What level of expectations for your coach, yourself, your training, your nutrition, your rate of progress? You can't expect 10/10 outcomes with 6/10 inputs.
