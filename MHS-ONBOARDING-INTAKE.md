# MHS Onboarding Intake — v1 (2026-09-28)

**Standard:** seamless, efficient, clean, inquisitive. No question survives unless its answer changes the Blueprint. The intake must deliver six things: **goals · limitations · obstacles (real and imagined) · the 4 Capacities · willing & able · what he does now.**

**Total client time: 38 minutes across three sittings + one in-person session with Ben.** Every question accepts typed, voice memo, or upload. Nothing is asked that the application or the sales call already captured — those fields are pre-filled and shown for confirmation only.

**What we already know before he starts** (pre-filled from application + call, no re-ask): age, profession/arena, trigger, stated goal, cost of inaction, 12-month vision, what he has tried, decision style, preferred contact channel.

---

## Sitting 1 — The Standard · 12 minutes · Day 0–1

*Purpose: WANT + EXPECT. What he is here for and what a 10/10 looks like to him.*

**1.1 Goals** · 4 min
1. Confirm or correct: *"On your call you said you want [goal, verbatim]. Is that still the target?"*
2. *"What is the goal behind that goal — what does reaching it let you do or stop worrying about?"*
3. *"Rank these for the next 12 months, most important first: how you look · how you feel · how you perform · how long you stay sharp."* (drag-rank)
4. *"What would make this a 10/10 at day 90, in your own words?"*

**1.2 Expectations and communication** · 4 min
5. *"How do you want us to communicate with you?"* — text / WhatsApp / email / voice · short and direct / detailed and referenced
6. *"How directly can we call you out?"* — Say it straight, every time / Straight, but tell me why / Push, then let me think / Ask before you push
7. *"What do you do when you're being lazy — and what should we do about it?"* (free text)
8. *"What do you expect from us that you didn't get from anyone you've worked with before?"*

**1.3 Life and profession** · 4 min
9. Confirm: *"[Profession/arena from application]. Right?"*
10. *"Is your work right now purposeful, challenging, heavy, or manageable? Pick the closest."*
11. *"Next 90 days: travel, deals, seasons, events already on the calendar that will collide with this."* (free text or calendar upload)
12. *"Is anyone in your life not fully on board with you doing this?"* — Everyone's behind it / One person is neutral / One person is against it / I haven't told anyone

→ **Outputs:** WANT (goal + goal-behind-goal + ranked priorities), EXPECT (comms contract, call-out permission, laziness protocol), calendar constraints (ACTUAL), people constraint (ACTUAL).

## Sitting 2 — Capacity · 16 minutes · Day 1–3

*Purpose: the 4 Capacities as he experiences them today, plus what he does now. The objective side comes from diagnostics and Ben's gym session; this is the subjective baseline it gets compared against.*

**2.1 Psychological capacity — mindset and stress response** · 4 min
13. *"How easily do you get stressed? 1 = nothing rattles me, 10 = I run hot most days."*
14. *"How stressed do you actually feel right now, 1–10?"* (the gap between 13 and 14 is the resilience read)
15. *"Where is the stress coming from? Tick all that apply and star the biggest."* — Financial · Emotional / relationships · Work load · Environmental (home, toxins, travel) · Internal (something in my body doesn't feel right) · Not much stress
16. *"Your relationship with hard things — do you go toward them or around them? When does that flip?"* (free text)
17. *"How do you turn off?"* (free text)

**2.2 Physiological capacity — sleep and recovery** · 3 min
18. *"Typical bed time and wake time on a weekday. On a weekend."*
19. *"Nights per week you wake up and stay up more than 20 minutes."* — 0 / 1–2 / 3–4 / 5+
20. *"Do you wear anything that tracks sleep or HRV?"* — Oura / WHOOP / Garmin / Apple Watch / Other / None → if yes: *"Grant us access or upload your last 30 days."*
21. *"How many hard training sessions a week can you recover from right now before something gives?"* — 1–2 / 3 / 4 / 5+ / I don't know

**2.3 Physical capacity — training** · 5 min
22. *"Years of consistent training?"* — Under 2 / 2–5 / 5–10 / 10–20 / 20+
23. *"Tell me about a time you had an amazing, memorable workout. How did you feel when you were done?"* (free text — this defines what "great" means to him)
24. *"When you train, do you like to push or hate to push?"* — Love it, go till I drop / Like it, want to leave with energy / Do it because I should / Avoid it when I can
25. *"How many days a week do you want to train? How many minutes do you actually have per session on a normal week, not your best week?"*
26. *"Current and past injuries. Anything that hurts now, anything that has ever stopped you training."* (free text)
27. *"Where do you train?"* — Home gym / Commercial gym / Both / Travelling most weeks → equipment notes

**2.4 Metabolic capacity — nutrition, medical, supplements** · 4 min
28. *"Walk through a normal weekday of eating: when you eat and roughly what."* (free text or photo log)
29. *"On a scale of 1–10, how regimented are you with food? 1 = I eat what's in front of me, 10 = every meal is planned."*
30. *"When you eat off plan, what is it and how many times a week?"* (free text)
31. *"Allergies, intolerances, foods you won't eat."*
32. *"Everything you currently take: supplements, pharmaceuticals, peptides, hormones. Name and dose — a photo of the shelf is fine."* (upload)
33. *"Any diagnosis from a doctor, physical or mental, past or present. Any major illness or surgery."*
34. *"Labs or tests in the last 12 months?"* — Blood · Urine · Stool · DNA · DEXA / body scan · Other → upload each

→ **Outputs:** subjective baseline on all 4 Capacities, WHAT HE DOES NOW (training reality, eating pattern, full stack), wearables access, testing history for the PPLD roadmap, medical constraints (ACTUAL).

## Sitting 3 — Willing, Able, and the Obstacles · 10 minutes · Day 3–5

*Purpose: WILLING & ABLE envelope and the two constraint lists — perceived (beliefs we dismantle with data) and actual (realities we design around). Kept separate because they get opposite treatment.*

**3.1 Willing and able** · 4 min
35. *"The most time you will actually give this on a normal weekday: training, prep, sleep routine, everything."* — Under 45 min / 45–75 / 75–120 / 2 hrs+
36. *"What will you not give up? Client dinners, wine, late nights, a sport, a food. Say it without judgment — we would rather know."*
37. *"What have you sustained for 6+ months before? What have you always abandoned — and at what point did it break?"*
38. *"When life gets busy, what breaks first: training, nutrition, sleep, recovery, or everything at once? Why that one?"*

**3.2 Relationship with the things** · 3 min
*Principle: the relationship with a thing matters more than the thing.*
39. *"One line each — your honest relationship with: food · training · stress · your relationships · money."* (five short fields)

**3.3 Obstacles, real and imagined** · 3 min
40. *"What do you believe might hold you back here?"*
41. *"When you imagine this not working, what is the story you tell?"*
42. *"Is there anything about you that you think makes you a harder case — genetics, age, metabolism, history?"*
43. *"Home food reality: who cooks, who shops, how many meals a week are out of your hands?"*

→ **Outputs:** WILLING & ABLE envelope (time, non-negotiables, adherence history, break point), constraint category, relationship map, PERCEIVED constraints (40–42, coach dismantles with his own data as it lands), ACTUAL constraints (11, 12, 26, 33, 43 — Blueprint designs around them).

---

## Sitting 4 — In person with Ben · 60–75 minutes · Day 7–21

*The gym session. Every client. This is the objective Physical Capacity read and the relationship moment. Ben's existing physical assessment protocol applies (already built).*

Ben observes and records:
- Joint-by-joint movement: every plane, every range available? Clean = crisp and sharp, no pain, no compensation.
- Left–right balance (flagged as extremely important — score each pattern L/R).
- What he does well. What he does not.
- How he responds to being pushed (confirms or corrects Q24).
- Injury history walk-through in movement (confirms Q26).

Output: Physical Capacity grade, movement priorities, the first two things training must fix, and Ben's read on the man.

Rule carried from canon: **program design never waits on the full ten diagnostics.** Sittings 1–3 + Sitting 4 + the "Now" lab set are sufficient for the first protocol.

## What was deliberately left out
- Body weight / waist / photos as questions — captured by the hardware kit and the first weekly check-in instead.
- Anything already answered on the application or call — shown pre-filled, confirm only.
- Generic "health history" checklists — replaced by 33–34 (diagnoses + uploads).
- The word "cheat" — never used.
- Motivation / "why" questions — the application already did the work; re-asking reads as not having listened.

## Open for Ben
- Sitting count: three short sittings (recommended) vs. one 38-minute form.
- Whether Sitting 4 happens before or after the Blueprint Call (recommended: before, so Ben's read is in the Blueprint).
- Any question above he wants cut. Target is fewer, not more.
