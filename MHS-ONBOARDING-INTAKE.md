## SECTION 1 — WHAT YOU'RE BUILDING
*"Before we get into the systems, we want to be crystal clear on what we're actually building toward."*

**Q1.** What's the single most important thing you want to achieve in the next 90 days? *(Long-form text. Required.)*
The first domino. This is what your coach builds your Phase 1 protocol around. Be specific — "feel better" doesn't give us anything to work with. "Sleep through the night without waking at 3am, drop 10 pounds, and have steady energy through a 12-hour workday" gives us everything.

**Q2.** If we look 12 months out and we've nailed it — what's true about your body and your life that isn't true today? *(Long-form text. Required.)*
The vision. The coach uses this to sequence the year and decide which standards to introduce when.

**Q3.** If you think further out — 5 to 10 years — what does that version of you look like? *(Long-form text. Optional.)*
Optional because not every man thinks this far. But if you do, this is where the identity-level work lives. The coach uses it as the north star for the entire engagement.

## SECTION 2 — WHERE YOU ARE RIGHT NOW
*"The honest version. Not the ideal week — the actual one."*

**Q4.** What are your biggest complaints right now — physically, energetically, or mentally? *(Long-form text. Required.)*
The subjective signals labs won't catch. "I crash at 2pm." "My brain fog is killing my afternoon meetings." "My lower back is limiting everything." These get fixed first because they're what you feel every day.

**Q5.** What's the biggest physical limitation you're dealing with right now — pain, injury, restriction, range of motion? *(Long-form text. Required.)*
Critical safety input. Your coach won't write a training program that aggravates an existing limitation. Be specific about which side, what movements trigger it, when it started.

**Q6.** What's gotten in the way before? What could derail this now? *(Long-form text. Required.)*
Patterns of failure are more useful than a list of past attempts. Travel, work intensity, family demands, energy crashes, motivation patterns, environment, all of it.

## SECTION 3 — THE SELF-ASSESSMENT
*"A quick gut-check across 10 areas. No detail needed here — just an honest snapshot."*

**Q7.** Rate yourself across these 10 areas. Failing / Frustrated / Improving / Thriving. *(Button grid. Required.)*
Sleep · Stress management · Nutrition · Strength training · Cardiovascular fitness · Mobility & movement quality · Supplementation · Mindset & emotional state · Relationships · Sense of purpose & direction
Be honest. Wherever you mark Failing or Frustrated, that's information for your coach — not judgment. The areas where you're guessing or don't have data are usually the highest-leverage places to start.

## SECTION 4 — HOW YOU OPERATE
*"This shapes how your coach works with you from day one. There's no wrong answer — there's only the answer that's true for you."*

**Q8.** What kind of communication style works best for you? *(Single select. Required.)*
- Direct, no filter, call me on my bullshit. I want the truth without softening. Pushback is what makes me sharper. Don't manage my ego.
- Direct but considered. Honest feedback delivered with context. I want the truth, but I also want to understand the reasoning behind it.
- Collaborative and curious. I want to be brought along, asked questions, given the chance to think it through with you. Top-down directives don't land for me.
- Steady and supportive. I respond to encouragement and consistency. Harsh delivery shuts me down. The fastest path with me is patience and clarity.

**Q9.** When you fall off track — and you will, everyone does — how do you want us to handle it? *(Single select. Required.)*
- Call me out the same day. I want immediate accountability. Silence makes me drift.
- Flag it once, then give me space. I want to know you noticed, but then let me self-correct. If it becomes a pattern, escalate.
- Address it at our next check-in. Don't make a thing of it in the moment. Keep it in context and we'll talk through it on schedule.
- Help me understand why. I don't need to be called out — I need help diagnosing what happened so it doesn't repeat.

**Q10.** When life gets chaotic — travel, work crisis, family emergency — what tends to happen to the plan? *(Single select. Required.)*
- I abandon it entirely and restart later. When things get hard, the plan is the first thing to go.
- I simplify to the bare minimum. I'll do something, even if it's just one workout and a clean meal a day.
- I try to force everything to fit. I keep the full plan and grind through it anyway.
- I lose the plan but maintain the principles. The structure breaks but the habits hold.

This determines whether your coach pre-builds a chaos protocol, gives you permission to scale back, or designs around your natural pattern.

**Q11.** How often do you want to communicate with your coach? *(Single select. Required.)*
- Daily. Quick check-ins, daily voice notes, real-time problem-solving. I want a constant operating partner.
- A few times a week. Touch points throughout the week, but not a constant feed.
- Weekly with a strong call. One scheduled deep conversation per week, minimal in between.
- Async and structured. Set the protocol, let me execute, check in at scheduled intervals. Don't ping me.

Wrong cadence = friction from day one. We match this exactly.
