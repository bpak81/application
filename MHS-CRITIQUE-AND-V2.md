## PART A — The critique. What's weak, phase by phase.

### A0. Cross-cutting — the five hardest problems

**1. The map advertises assets that don't exist yet.** The journey shows "The Approach" emails, the non-buyer game-plan PDF, the welcome letter, the stress diagnostic form, and the AI triage as if live. Status: the persuasion email layer was ruled **v1 scaffolding, not ship-ready** (Ben, Jul 12); the PDF and letter are unbuilt; triage is a spec. A man moving through the funnel today hits three silent dead zones (phases 3, 5, 7-support). **The funnel is sellable today only from application → call → close; everything after close leans on Sara and improvisation.** Build order must be: nurture emails → welcome letter → non-buyer PDF → triage. Nothing else matters until those exist.

**2. The "credit card ready" chain has a missing link.** Requirement (Sep 28): he arrives at the call understanding who we are, what we do, why he should sign up. The application *reads* him brilliantly but *teaches* him almost nothing — by design (4 minutes). The teaching was assigned to the 3-video drip (skipped — one recall video now does three jobs: self-identification, kill-the-current-strategy, show-up priming) and the 6 emails (not ship-ready). So today the entire education burden sits on **one generic video**. That is the single biggest conversion risk in the system, and it's invisible because the map shows the phase as "exists."

**3. The gym session with Ben doesn't survive contact with geography.** "Every client" trains with Ben during onboarding days 7–21. Most clients won't live in driving distance. Unanswered: does he fly in? Does Ben travel? Is this a mandatory trip baked into the offer (actually a selling point at $84K — "you will train with Ben in week two")? Or is there a remote fallback (video movement screen with a coach) that quietly makes "every client" false? **Pick one and write it into the offer and the welcome letter.** Also a capacity question: at 25+ clients/month this is Ben's calendar bottleneck; D-14/D-15 scaling logic applies here too.

**4. "100% success" is claimed but not instrumented.** The guarantee engine is defined (DT5 → daily win → arithmetic), but nowhere is the measurement wired: no named funnel metrics (application completion %, booking %, show %, close %, onboarding-complete-by-day-21 %, DT5 adherence distribution, month-4 churn flag) and no definition of what counts as a client "success" at 12 months. If the claim is the brand, the dashboard is the proof. The weekly sheet captures client data; nothing captures *system* data.

**5. One name appears in every bottleneck.** Ben: the gym session, Ben-direct 2-call sales, the $1K genetics consult, the who-needs-love scan, final say on 18 open register items. The system's stated goal is a machine; the current design is a machine with Ben as a load-bearing wall in five places. Each is individually fine; together they cap scale and make every vacation a service risk. Decide which two of the five are *actually* Ben-only (my read: gym session + who-needs-love) and delegate or productize the rest.

### A1. Application
- **The honesty of the frame.** "We take ten new men per month" — only keep if true per capacity ruling (4/25/100 by tier is the canon; one flagship now = what number?). At this price, a caught exaggeration is fatal. Same for "we don't reschedule no-shows" — enforced, or theater? If a $500-paid founder no-shows once, you will reschedule him. Write the policy you'll actually keep.
- **Q2/Q3 partial overlap.** The identity mirror and the bottleneck both let him say "no system/integration." Tolerable (different FIRE jobs), but the decoder must not double-count one signal as two.
- **Voice input missing.** Onboarding accepts typed/voice/upload; the application is typed-only. The two highest-value free-texts (Q6 tried-before, Q10 commitment) would be richer spoken. Add voice on those two.
- **The spouse is read, then ignored.** Q12 captures the decision unit; the canon has a spouse one-pager/2-min video in the objection cards — but nothing in the flow sends it. If Q12 ≠ "entirely mine," the pre-call sequence should offer the partner asset *before* the call, not as objection cleanup after.
- **"Not sure yet" → Nurture is a blunt gate.** A man 10/10 serious but mid-acquisition answers honestly and vanishes into a sequence that isn't built. Add a second routing lane: high-signal-but-later → a dated advisor follow-up, not generic nurture.
- **No social proof anywhere in the flow.** Deliberate discretion ("you'll never see our clients named") — but the booking page asks for $500 on zero third-party evidence. The testimonial bank (doc 08) exists. One anonymized outcome line on the calendar screen ("Last quarter's average client: −9% bodyfat, +22% HRV" — if true) keeps discretion and adds proof.

### A2. Booking / Audit

### A3. Pre-call nurture

### A4. Game Plan Call

Recommendation: selection posture out loud, conversion mechanics underneath — and the advisor never says a sentence that would embarrass the selective frame if quoted back.

- **Gaps against Ben's new taxonomy** — see Part B; the real ones: family history (nowhere), nervous-system/mindset state as a named read (scattered), relationship-with-the-outcome (nowhere), 36-month target (spoken on the sales call, never written down), communication *frequency* (type captured, cadence not).

### A7. Blueprint Call

## PART C — "Defining Success" — placement and build

Ben's module (verbatim intent, cleaned): *How do you define success — and whose definition is it? Not mine, not society's, not your parents', not your friends'. Find it for you — usually somewhere along peace, satisfaction, significance, contribution. Why is this important to you, why now? What are you actually chasing — goals, or the avoidance of suffering? What are your personal standards? What does winning look like — look, feel, or perform — and how do you know when you've won? Short-term vs. long-term success. What level of expectation do you have for your coach, yourself, your training, your nutrition, your rate of progress? You can't expect 10/10 outcomes with 6/10 inputs. The path to fulfillment and purpose is formed in silence and forged under pressure.*

**This is call material, not form material.** Written forms flatten philosophy into homework; this module's power is a coach asking "whose definition is it?" and sitting in the silence after. Placement:

1. **Blueprint Call, Move 1 (expanded to ~15 min): "The Success Definition."** Before the data reveal. Script beats: define success → whose definition → chasing vs. avoiding → personal standards → what winning looks like (look/feel/perform) → how you'll know you've won → short vs. long term. Output written into the client file as the preamble above W4 — **WANT now has two layers: the outcome, and the definition of success it serves.** Read back with the four yeses (W4 stays W4; success definition is WANT's first paragraph).
2. **Seeded in Sitting 1 (2 light questions, no philosophy):** "How will you know, concretely, when you've won?" + "Whose standard are you measuring yourself against today?" — primes the call, costs ~1 min.
3. **The 10/10-outcomes line closes the Blueprint Call's contract move** — it's the client-facing voice of the existing 10-in/10-out contract. Verbatim: *"You can't expect 10/10 outcomes with 6/10 inputs."*
4. **Content asset:** the whole module is a pre-call video/email script in pure Ben voice ("Success is not how I define it…"). When the nurture layer gets built, this is email 2 or video 1. It does the missing education job from A0-2 while pre-framing the call.

**The cumulative principle (Ben, Oct 28: "onboarding/the calls should be cumulative for information gathering") — codified as a design law:**

> **The Cumulative File rule.** One client file. Every touchpoint *reads* from it before asking anything and *writes* to it after. Nothing is ever asked twice; something known is always read back. Every conversation opens with proof the last one was heard: the Game Plan Call opens on P1 verbatim · the recap email quotes the call · the sittings arrive pre-filled · the Blueprint Call opens on the 30-day-old vision + the fast-win status · every weekly check-in's Block 1 references last week · the monthly roadmap opens on the ladder deltas · the quarterly review opens on the 90-day objectives he wrote and dated. Any new touchpoint added to the system must name what it reads and what it writes before it ships.

This was implicit in the canon; it is now a stated law and the first test applied to every future asset.
