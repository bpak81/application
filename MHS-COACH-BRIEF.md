## SECTION 1 — WHAT YOU'RE BUILDING · ~3 min

*Frame:* **Before we get into the systems, we want to be crystal clear on what we're actually building toward.**

**B1 — What's the single most important thing you want to achieve in the next 90 days?** *(required)*
*The first domino. This is what your coach builds your Phase 1 protocol around. Be specific — "feel better" doesn't give us anything to work with. "Sleep through the night without waking at 3am, drop 10 pounds, and have steady energy through a 12-hour workday" gives us everything.*

**B2 — If we look 12 months out and we've nailed it — what's true about your body and your life that isn't true today?** *(required)*
*The vision. Your coach uses this to sequence the year and decide which standards to introduce when.*

**B3 — If you think further out — 5 to 10 years — what does that version of you look like?** *(optional)*
*Not every man thinks this far. If you do, this is the north star for the entire engagement.*

---

## SECTION 2 — WHERE YOU ARE RIGHT NOW · ~4 min

*Frame:* **The honest version. Not the ideal week — the actual one.**

**B4 — Write down the moment you knew something had to change.** *(required)*
*The photo. The number on a screen. The thing someone said. The mirror. We'll start the call there.*

**B5 — What are your biggest complaints right now — physically, energetically, or mentally?** *(required)*
*The subjective signals labs won't catch. "I crash at 2pm." "Brain fog is killing my afternoon meetings." "My lower back is limiting everything." These get fixed first, because they're what you feel every day.*

**B6 — What's the biggest physical limitation you're dealing with — pain, injury, restriction, range of motion?** *(required; "none" is an answer)*
*Critical safety input. Your coach won't write a program that aggravates an existing limitation. Be specific: which side, what movements trigger it, when it started.*

**B7 — What's gotten in the way before? What could derail this now?** *(required)*
*Patterns beat lists. Travel, work intensity, family demands, energy crashes, motivation, environment — whatever has actually broken your consistency, and whatever could break it this time.*

## SECTION 3 — THE SELF-ASSESSMENT · ~45 sec

*Frame:* **A quick gut-check across 10 areas. No detail — just an honest snapshot.**

**B8 — Rate yourself: Failing / Frustrated / Improving / Thriving.** *(button grid, required)*
Sleep · Stress management · Nutrition · Strength training · Cardiovascular fitness · Mobility & movement quality · Supplementation · Mindset & emotional state · Relationships · Sense of purpose & direction

*Be honest. Wherever you mark Failing or Frustrated, that's information for your coach — not judgment. The areas where you're guessing or don't have data are usually the highest-leverage places to start.*
